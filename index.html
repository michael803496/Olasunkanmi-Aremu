Law, governance and grassroots service

Olasunkanmi Aremu Olaleye was called to the Nigerian Bar in 1993. He holds an LL.B (1992) and an LL.M (2017) from the University of Lagos, Akoka, and hails from Iresaadu in Surulere Local Government Area of Oyo State.

He built a private practice across civil, commercial and electoral matters, then moved into public service, where he has led institutions that demand neutrality, legal precision and firm administrative control.

He is recognised for his calm temperament, administrative discipline and ability to manage complex institutions without controversy.
